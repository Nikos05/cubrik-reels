## 4. The strategy — 2:30 → 3:50

**[A]** Now, the strategy. This is the part you'll want to screenshot.

**[DEMO]** Gold only, XAU USD. It's a smart money strategy, with a news filter on top.

**[DEMO]** First, the bias. On the four hour chart, the AI reads the market structure: if price is breaking highs, we only look for buys. If it's breaking lows, only sells.

**[DEMO]** Second, the liquidity. During the London open and the New York open, the AI waits for price to sweep the Asian session high or low, or the previous day's high or low. That's where retail stop losses sit.

**[DEMO]** Third, the entry. After the sweep, it waits for a change of character on the fifteen minute chart, then enters on the retest of the fair value gap or the order block left by that move.

**[DEMO]** The stop goes just beyond the wick of the sweep. Half the position is closed at twice the risk, the stop moves to break even, and the rest runs to the next pool of liquidity.

**[B]** And the news filter. No new trade thirty minutes before or after high impact US news like CPI, jobs or the Fed. And the AI reads the headlines before every decision: if something big just hit gold, it skips.

**[DEMO]** Risk was set high on purpose for this test: two percent per trade, a maximum of two trades a day, and a hard daily loss limit of three percent. On a real account, you'd start much lower.
