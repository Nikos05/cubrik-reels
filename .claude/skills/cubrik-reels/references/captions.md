# Instagram caption format

All finished captions live in `captions.md` at the repo root — add new ones there, same structure:

1. Hook line + one emoji (🧡) — echoes the video hook, not a copy of it.
2. One sentence: what cubrik does here, always "connected to Claude".
3. Three numbered steps (what you do in Claude).
4. Safety line: "Nothing goes live without your OK." (+ stops / daily loss when relevant).
5. CTA: `Comment "WORD"` — same word as the end card. Released feature → "and I'll send you the setup guide";
   **unreleased feature → "coming soon" in line 2 and "early access as soon as it's live" in the CTA.**
6. Legal: "Not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results."
   (FR: « Pas un conseil financier. Les performances passées ne préjugent pas des résultats futurs. »)
7. 6–8 hashtags, mixed broad/niche (#claude #claudeai #trading #tradingbot #ai …).

Never: income claims, "guaranteed", win-rate promises, "official Anthropic partner".
US audience → English captions, American spelling, US prime time scheduling.
